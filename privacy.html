Privacy Notice

This site does not collect, store, or process any personal data.

  • No cookies are set.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts are used.
  • No accounts, logins, or user data are stored.
  • Server access logging is disabled. No visitor IP addresses or request logs are recorded.
  • Any data you enter (e.g. a GPX file) is processed only in your browser and is not transmitted to or stored on the server, except where required to fetch results from a third-party API (weather data from Open-Meteo). See Open-Meteo's own privacy policy for details on how they handle requests.
  • Map tiles are loaded directly from CARTO's CDN (cartocdn.com). As with any externally hosted map or image, your browser contacts their servers directly to load tiles, which may involve standard request logging (e.g. IP address) on their end. See CARTO's privacy notice for details.

If you have questions about this notice, you can reach me at hello@funcnroll.dev.
